# **Server Rules**  

1. **Respect and Courtesy**  
   - Treat all members with respect. Harassment, threats, bullying, or any form of aggression is strictly prohibited.  

2. **No Spam or Advertising**  
   - Avoid spamming chats or sharing unsolicited advertisements without admin approval.  

3. **Prohibited Content**  
   - No extremist, violent, pornographic, hateful, or otherwise illegal content is allowed.  

4. **Stay on Topic**  
   - Keep discussions relevant to the channel's purpose.  

5. **Forbidden Activities**  
   - Exploiting bugs, using cheats, or any tools to gain an unfair advantage is not allowed.  

6. **Admin Authority**  
   - Admins and moderators have the final say. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it respectfully in private messages.  

7. **Privacy**  
   - Do not share personal information of other members without their consent.  

8. **General Rules**  
   - Follow Discord's Terms of Service and the laws of your country.

9. **Politics**

10. **No Ad**
